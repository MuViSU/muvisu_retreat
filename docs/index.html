MuViSU Retreat 2026

4-6 November, Mont Fleur, Stellenbosch

Welcome to the retreat of MuViSU, the Centre for Multi-dimensional Data Visualisation at Stellenbosch University. See github.com/MuViSU for more about the organisation and its materials.

The goals of this retreat are:

  • Strengthen relationships and build awareness of individual group members’ skills
  • Make real progress (or solve) the identified research task / activity from a current project you are working on.
  • Optional: If you do not have a current project to work on, consider to develop and document applications that showcase the benefit of the Centre’s software.

Participant activity

Each participant should plan to bring a research activity to work on during the retreat, or be prepared to work with one or two other participants on their proposed activity. Think about what might be achieved in the 1.5 day time frame, and what might be an activity where getting input from others will be especially useful.

What to expect

The retreat is a chance to step away from your usual routine and work alongside others in a relaxed setting. You can look forward to:

  • Networking: meet researchers from across the group and build connections that last beyond the event.
  • Focused time: protected time to work and discuss ideas with others, without the usual interruptions.
  • Problem solving together: bring a small task from a larger project and work with others in the group who can offer feedback.
  • Nature: the venue is set in the Stellenbosch Winelands, so there’s time to get outside and enjoy the surroundings.
  • Fun: shared meals, downtime and plenty of chances to get to know each other.
